Gather — three visual directions

Same six screens, three personalities. All built on deep ultramarine, one gold accent, large tap targets, and emoji-led content. Pick one and I'll build the whole app against it.

6 screens × 3 directions Light + dark Reveal moment treated as the hero
DIRECTION 01

Ultramarine Classic

The safest, most confident read of the brief: one deep ultramarine field, gold used only where it earns attention, soft raised cards. Works for a 19-year-old at a concert and a 55-year-old at an office party. Gold primary buttons make the two actions on the landing screen impossible to miss.

DIRECTION 02

Aurora Glass

The Gen-Z read: near-black base with soft purple and blue aurora blooms, frosted glass cards, gradient gold buttons with a glow. Larger radii, more air, a floating halo behind the reveal. This is the one that photographs well in a demo video — but it leans harder on device GPU, so verify blur performance on a cheap Android before committing.

DIRECTION 03

Daylight Editorial

The office-credible read. Off-white paper, ultramarine as the working colour, gold held back for the countdown and the reveal. Big tight display type on the landing, the current step inverted into a solid navy block so it dominates the Now screen. Reads well in daylight, prints well in a README, and is the easiest of the three to keep legible for the older end of the audience.

What holds across all three

  • Gold is the only accent. It marks the current step, the live count, and the winner — nothing else.
  • 56px primary buttons, 50px vote targets. Every screen passes the "thumb in one hand on a bus" test.
  • Now is the first tab. Current step, next step, progress, and headcount answer "where are we?" without scrolling.
  • Emoji do the work. Avatars, reaction bursts, and identity — no profile photos to upload.
  • The reveal is the only loud screen. Everything else stays quiet so that moment lands.
  • Plain error copy. "We couldn't find a room with that code." Never "Oops!"
